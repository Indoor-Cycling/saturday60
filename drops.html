Saturday 60

Drops Library Editor

Open a Saturday60-Drops file to read or change it outside the Selector and the Build Sheet. Times are shown in m:ss — type them that way and they are converted back on save.

How matching works. The tools find a song by its title with the artist, capitalisation and punctuation ignored — so “Titanium” and “Titanium — David Guetta, Sia” are the same song. Rename a title here and its key is rebuilt on save.
Drop times take m:ss separated by commas (1:14, 2:40). Plain seconds work too. Drill is the interval text the Build Sheet puts in Cue Notes.
