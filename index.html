To install on iPhone or iPad:

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) in Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. A “Sat 60” icon appears with your other apps.

Bringing your old data across

Anything saved in the standalone HTML files you were opening before does not follow you here — a browser keeps that data separately for every file.

Open your old copy once, press Backup, then open the tool here and press Restore and pick that file. Do it for the Selector and the Build Sheet and you are moved in.

All three tools share one storage area now, so a drops library saved in the Selector is the same one the Build Sheet sees.
Add to Home Screen once and it opens full-screen, with no address bar and no internet needed.
