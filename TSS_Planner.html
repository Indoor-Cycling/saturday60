Build a hard set — spread the work across the choruses

Efforts ramp up, rests shrink into the finish (equal after Z5, 2× after Z6, 3× after Z7), so riders empty out on the song’s biggest moment — not one interval before it. “Add to the plan” drops the whole set into the builder above.

How the score works. TSS = 100 × (minutes ÷ 60) × (intensity ÷ FTP)², summed over every interval, using each zone’s midpoint as its intensity. A full hour at FTP = 100 TSS.
First-60 vs full file. MOWL scores the whole file including cool-down, so both are shown; the cap applies to the first 60 minutes. MOWL usually reads a point or two below this math.
Per-set discipline. Watch the per-segment subtotals and the ⚠ flags — the point is that every set is something riders can actually finish, not just that the hour adds up.
