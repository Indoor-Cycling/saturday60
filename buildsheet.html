Section library

Build a section whenever inspiration strikes, tap its header's Save button to shelve it as a named block, then assemble a ride here later: pick a destination section and Replace its rows (or Append to them). The library lives on this device and travels inside sheet codes.

Rules that never bend: RPM is on-beat or half-beat only · 110 RPM hard ceiling on every interval · songs faster than 110 BPM ride half-beat or get capped · music leads, clock follows on work segments · warm-up, bridges & cool-down keep clean clock time.
Session Designer: build the Spotify playlist first in ride order (crossfade 10 s, gapless, Automix off), auto-import intervals per song, set counts from the Intervals column, nudge boundaries to the Drop times.

Import a Spotify playlist export

Best source: Chosic's Playlist Analyzer (chosic.com/spotify-playlist-analyzer — paste the playlist link, then download the CSV) because its export includes each song's BPM, which fills in automatically. Exportify (exportify.app) and Chosic's plain exporter also work but carry titles only. Upload the file or paste its contents below. Songs fill the sheet in ride order, top to bottom. Sanity-check imported BPMs that look doubled or halved — the tap-tester settles any argument.
