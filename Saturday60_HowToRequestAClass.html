THE SATURDAY 60 — How to Ask Claude for a Class

Plain words work. Name what matters this week, and everything you leave out falls back to the defaults in the right-hand column of section 3. Say “this week” for a one-off and “from now on” to change the rulebook. The rules that always ride along: RPM beat-locked (1:1 or ½) · 110 RPM ceiling · never stand above 85 RPM · no explicit versions · a gear change only with a zone change · PLAYLIST FIRST — MUSIC LEADS

4 — CHANGING A CLASS AFTER IT’S BUILT
The changeSay it like this
1Swap a song Swap Burn for something in the same BPM band · Replace song 7 with Believer
2Move a song Move Valerie to the Finisher · Put Hey Brother before Higher Love
3Easier or harder Bring the TSS down to 68 · A harder climb, same total · An easier warm-up — when it’s over the cap, the warm-up, builds and recoveries ease first; the big finishes are cut last.
4Fix a drop Rolling in the Deep: chorus 1 starts at 1:15.6, bar 33.1 — your bar readings beat the lyric timings.
5Re-time to the real tracks Attach an Exportify CSV of the playlist: exact lengths and Explicit flags. Needed whenever a version or length differs.
6Change the layout Rebuild Nov 21 as eight segments · Drop the bridges
7Change the bridge music Swap the Cheers bridge for the Jeopardy think music
8Apply a new rule to an old class Redo Nov 14 under the gear rule
9One cue’s wording Quicker to do yourself: edit the words after the zone in that song’s Intervals cell on the Build Sheet.
How to point at things. Blocks by name — Warm-Up, Endurance / Tempo, Bridge 1, Hill Climb, Bridge 2, Sprints, Finisher, Cool-Down. Songs by title or by the Build Sheet’s # column (“song 7”). Effort as a zone (Z1–Z7) or an RPE (1–10; the RPE card maps 1–2 = Z1, 3–4 = Z2, 5 = Z3, 6–7 = Z4, 8 = Z5, 9 = Z6, 10 = Z7). Times either in the song (“0:59 into Rolling in the Deep”) or on the ride clock (“32:57” — the MOWL time on the Cue Print and the Build Sheet’s Σ). Say which one.
5 — WHAT CLAUDE NEEDS FROM YOU, AND WHAT YOU DO AFTER
Before
1The date and the layout (Claude asks for whichever is missing).
2Your songs — or you pick.
3For the Spotify playlist: the Claude desktop app open on your computer, signed in to open.spotify.com in its built-in browser. Claude adds the tracks in order and reads the playlist back to check them.
4An Exportify CSV when Claude asks for one (exact lengths, Explicit flags).
After
1Spotify: make the playlist Public and download it for offline.
2Build Sheet: Backup, then Import ← Build Sheet and paste the code.
3MOWL: upload the file at login.mowl.com/profile/import, link the music, nudge the work intervals onto the drops, split at the bridges.
4Excel: Export for Excel → paste at B2 on My Playlist → FormatPlaylistPrint (Playlist Print + Cue Print).
6 — THIS WEEK, OR FROM NOW ON
Just this class Anything said with a date or this week changes only that class.
Every class after From now on… · Going forward… · Until I say otherwise… — Claude changes the rulebook and tells you what it changed.
Fixed until you say so RPM beat-locked (1:1 or ½) · 110 RPM ceiling · never stand above 85 RPM · no explicit versions · a gear change only with a zone change · hold limits Z5 5:00, Z6 3:00, Z7 0:30 · the BPM band for each block · TSS cap 75.
