THE SATURDAY 60 — Getting Started

Three tools for building a 60-minute indoor cycling class around the music: a BPM selector, a ride build sheet, and a drops library. Free, no account, nothing uploaded anywhere.
There is nothing to download or install from a store. It is a web address that turns into an app.

1 · OPEN IT

https://indoor-cycling.github.io/saturday60/ Open that on your phone or your computer. That is the whole thing — you are already using it.

2 · MAKE IT AN APP (30 SECONDS, OPTIONAL BUT WORTH IT)
AndroidChrome shows an Install prompt, or menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. It then opens from your home screen like any other app, with no browser bar.
iPhone / iPadIn Safari, tap Share (the square with the arrow) → Add to Home Screen.
ComputerChrome or Edge: the install icon at the right of the address bar, or menu → Install. It lands in your Start menu or Applications folder.
Why botherInstalled, it works with no signal — in a studio basement, on a plane, anywhere. Nothing is fetched while you use it.
3 · WHAT THE THREE TOOLS DO
Song BPM SelectorPhone-first. Tap in a tempo or type it, see where that song fits and what cadence it beat-locks to, mark the drop times as the track plays, and colour-code them. Send the playlist to the Build Sheet.
MOWL Build SheetDesktop-first. The ride block by block: songs, lengths, RPM, positions, drills, power and heart-rate cues. It does the clock maths — you see the class land on 60 minutes as you build it. Prints to a sheet you can ride from.
Drops LibraryEvery drop time you have ever marked, kept by song. Mark a track once and it fills itself in every week you use that track again.
4 · THINGS WORTH KNOWING ON DAY ONE
1Your data is yours and it is local. Everything lives in the browser on your own device. There is no account, no server, nothing uploaded, and nobody else can see your rides — including whoever gave you this sheet.
2It starts empty. Your drops library, drills and playlists are your own. Nothing arrives with the app.
3Set your own numbers first. On the Build Sheet, the header line has FTP and Max HR boxes. Type yours in; the zone table recalculates. Then press Refresh targets to rescale the per-song power and heart-rate cues to you.
4Back it up now and then. Each tool has a Backup button that writes a dated file, and a Restore that reads it. Deleting the installed app can clear its storage on some phones — a backup file is the only copy that survives that.
5Phone and desktop are separate. Same app, but each device keeps its own data. The transfer codes and backup files are how work moves between them.
6Updates arrive on their own. When the app changes, a bar slides up offering Update now. Your data is never touched by an update.

Built for Keiser M3 / M3i bikes and the MOWL (Intelligent Cycling) platform, but the tempo, phrase and clock maths apply to any bike. Everything is built around one idea: PLAYLIST FIRST — MUSIC LEADS. Pick the songs, then let their structure decide where the efforts go — a drill that starts on a drop feels inevitable; one that starts mid-phrase never does.
